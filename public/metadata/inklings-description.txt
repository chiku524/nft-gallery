Inklings are painted, not pixelated.

A 5,555-piece collection of looping ink-wash PFP portraits on Ink, Kraken’s Ethereum layer 2. Each Inkling is stacked from six painterly layers — paper, bloom, visage, gaze, mark, and adorn — then flattened onto one 16-frame GIF. Dye drifts. Eyes blink. Soft edges only.

Minting on Ink (chain ID 57073). Gas is ETH. 0.006 ETH to mint.
